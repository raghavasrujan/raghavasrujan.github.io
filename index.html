    Until Sep 2026

    I built agents that do the audit legwork

    At Infosys I built Audit AI, a production multi-agent platform for a Big Four audit client. Auditors upload their evidence and the list of samples to test in one go, and a line of specialised agents takes it from there.

    One classifies each document, one extracts it into structured data, one maps every sample to its support, one vouches the evidence against the audit procedure, and one writes the Excel report. I built two of them: Availability and Classification.

    Availability

    Finding the right page in the pile

    The Availability agent answers one question for every audit sample: which documents prove it? I built it on Corrective RAG. Hybrid search and semantic reranking on Azure AI Search pull the candidates, and a corrective step checks the retrieval before anything is trusted.

    It cut sample-to-document matching time by 60%, at over 90% accuracy.

    Classification

    Every answer gets a second look

    The Classification agent decides what each document is. Rather than tuning its prompts by hand, I optimised them systematically with DSPy.

    Then I added a Reviewer agent. It grades every output and sends the flagged ones back to be refined, and that review-and-refine loop improved output accuracy by up to 30%.

    Event-driven

    Stop asking, start listening

    The platform used to poll: every service kept asking whether its work was ready yet. I re-architected it around Azure Service Bus, so each step now reacts the moment the one before it finishes, and the whole system became more responsive.

    Buildathon 2026

    GemLearn

    GemLearn took first prize in Learning & Development at the Google Cloud & Infosys TOPAZ Buildathon. With one teammate I built GemLearn, which turns raw company documents (PDFs, specs, runbooks, decks) into role-personalised, difficulty-graded training: lessons, quizzes, mind-maps and timed exams in about three minutes.

    Under the hood, a five-stage Gemini pipeline checks its own grounding, routes work between Gemini 2.5 Pro and Flash to keep costs down, and runs on six Pub/Sub worker topics on Cloud Run. It writes the base of a 28-lesson course in about two to three minutes.
